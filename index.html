About Me

> ABOUT ME

I'm an AI engineer finishing an MS in Data Analytics Engineering (AI focus) at Northeastern University with a 4.0 GPA. I build statistical and deep learning models for real business problems and ship them as APIs and web apps.

At CREWASIS I built the client-facing agent that started the company's shift to agentic AI, now used on 20+ briefs from global consumer brands and pharma clients, and a systematic-review (SRR) service that turns scientific papers into a cited evidence matrix. Earlier, at Random Trees, my vision-language pipeline cut manual work by 60%.

Outside work I built HuskySwap, a Northeastern marketplace with 200+ users, and as a TA I mentor four master's project teams in ML and AI agents.
