About Me

> ABOUT ME

I'm an AI/ML engineer finishing an MS in Data Analytics Engineering with an AI focus at Northeastern University. I build LLM systems that hold up outside a notebook, like a self-hosted Qwen3-4B text-to-SQL agent that reaches 87.4% execution accuracy at 1/14th the cost.

At CREWASIS I build LLM systems for real client workflows: an agentic recommender that maps client briefs to 19 analysis models, and an automated systematic review (SRR) pipeline with local inference on Ollama. Earlier, at Random Trees, my LLM extraction pipeline cut manual document processing by 60%.

A background in marketing and go-to-market means I build with the end user in mind.
